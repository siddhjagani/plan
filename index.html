The flow of the getaway

A little adventure.
A lot of breathing room.

The itinerary balances travel, time at the villa, outdoor experiences and proper downtime. The final sequence can flex around the chosen property's check-in arrangements and local conditions.

Day one

Arrive & unwind

Ease into the getaway. Settle in, explore the property and let the outside world fade away.

  • Gather & travelMeet at the agreed pickup point and head towards Pawna together.
  • Welcome to the villaCheck in, choose rooms, freshen up and get familiar with the space.
  • Lunch & downtimeShare a meal, relax and enjoy the property.
  • Pool & lawn timeTake a dip in the villa pool, play a casual game or simply lounge.
  • Sunset by the lakeMake time for photographs and a relaxed group catch-up.
  • Welcome eveningDinner, music, conversations and easy-going games at the villa.
Day two

Explore & connect

The main experience day. Pick an outdoor adventure, then come back together for a full villa afternoon and evening.

  • Slow breakfastStart with breakfast and a relaxed morning at the property.
  • Choose your outingOptional kayaking or boating with a local operator, or a guided walk / Tikona Fort outing for those who want to explore.
  • Regroup for lunchReturn to the villa and enjoy a proper meal together.
  • Free afternoonPool time, board games, music, photography or a much-needed nap.
  • Team challengesLight-hearted games, a photo challenge or friendly team-versus-team activities.
  • Signature team eveningA shared dinner followed by music, karaoke, stories and a bonfire if the property permits it.
Day three

One last slow morning

No rushed start. Enjoy the setting, pack up and make the return journey together.

  • Sunrise & fresh airOptional lakeside walk, photographs and quiet time.
  • Breakfast togetherOne last meal at the villa and a relaxed catch-up.
  • Final group photosCapture the team, the property and the view before leaving.
  • Check out & departPack, check the rooms and leave the property as agreed.
  • Return journeyTravel back together, with a meal stop along the way if needed.
✳
Keep it flexible. The lake outing and fort visit are optional experiences, not fixed commitments. The group can choose what suits the weather, local operator availability and everyone's comfort.

Getting from the city to the lake

From Bhayandar.
Across Mumbai. Into Pawna.

A rail-first route keeps the group journey straightforward. The exact service will be chosen after the travel day is confirmed and the live timetable is checked.

01BhayandarWestern Railway
02DadarChange railway zone
03LonavalaCentral Railway
04Pawna villaPre-arranged road transfer
STEP 01
🚆

Bhayandar → Dadar Western

Board a Western Line local heading towards Churchgate and get down at Dadar Western. Before boarding, check the destination and stopping pattern on the station indicator so the selected service actually stops at Dadar.

Railway zone: Western Railway · Station code: DDR
STEP 02
↔

Interchange at Dadar

Use the foot overbridge and station signage to move from the Western Railway platforms to the Central Railway side. Dadar is a busy interchange, so keep a comfortable transfer buffer and regroup before boarding.

Follow signs for Central Railway / mainline trains.
STEP 03
🚄

Dadar Central → Lonavala

Board a scheduled Central Railway intercity or express train from Dadar that has Lonavala as a listed stop. This is not a regular Central Line suburban local all the way to Lonavala, so select the correct mainline service and confirm the stop before reserving seats.

Check the live train listing and platform before departure.
STEP 04
🚐

Lonavala → Pawna Lake

Meet the pre-arranged cab or group vehicle outside Lonavala station. Continue to the selected villa together. The last-mile route depends on which property the team chooses, so share the exact villa pin with the driver.

Keep the villa contact and driver details with the trip organiser.
Return journey

Reverse the same plan: villa pickup → Lonavala station → Central Railway train to Dadar → interchange to Western Railway → Bhayandar.

IRCTC train search ↗ NTES live train enquiry ↗ Open station route ↗

Before travel: once the travel day is decided, check the exact train, running days, Dadar stop, Lonavala arrival, reservation availability and return service. If there is no suitable Dadar departure, the organiser can compare a Central Railway departure from CSMT instead.

The little details

Good planning makes
space for good memories.

We'll keep the logistics simple and make sure the important details are sorted before the team sets off.

⌁

Getting there together

Travel as a group from an agreed pickup point, with the route and vehicle plan confirmed once the villa location is final.

  • Share pickup and return arrangements with everyone
  • Keep luggage and seating in mind
  • Plan a comfortable meal and refreshment stop
  • Keep the villa's check-in and check-out rules handy
✳

Meals & villa life

Make meals part of the experience: simple, fresh and easy for everyone to enjoy.

  • Coordinate breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner
  • Collect dietary needs before confirming the menu
  • Ask about vegetarian and Jain meal options where needed
  • Confirm drinking water, tea, coffee and kitchen access
A good trip is a safe trip

Enjoy the lake.
Respect the outdoors.

We'll keep activities sensible, use verified local providers and follow the villa's rules. The reservoir is not a swimming area; water activities should only happen through an organised operator with proper safety equipment.

Explore Maharashtra Tourism's Lonavala guide ↗
Lake activitiesUse a recognised operator, wear a life jacket and follow instructions. Never enter the reservoir to swim.
Fort outingChoose a suitable route, wear grippy footwear, carry water and skip the hike in unsafe weather.
Villa poolFollow pool rules, respect depth markings and avoid swimming alone.
Evening by the lakeStay within permitted areas and avoid walking close to the water after dark.
Bonfire & musicConfirm permission with the property and follow its sound and fire-safety rules.
Leave no traceKeep the property and shoreline clean; take all personal waste back with the group.
